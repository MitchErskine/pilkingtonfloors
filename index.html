Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our flooring services? Here are answers to the most common ones.

We install a wide range of flooring types including Amtico luxury vinyl tiles, solid and engineered hardwood, laminate, vinyl, carpet, and commercial flooring. As approved Amtico installers, we specialise in creating stunning designs with their premium range.

Most residential rooms can be completed in one to two days. Larger projects such as whole-house installations or commercial spaces typically take three to five days. We will provide a clear timeline during your free consultation.

Yes, we offer completely free, no-obligation quotes. Simply fill in our online quote form or give us a call, and we will arrange a convenient time to visit your property, take measurements, and discuss your requirements.

Amtico is a premium luxury vinyl tile brand known for its realistic wood and stone designs, exceptional durability, and easy maintenance. It is waterproof, scratch-resistant, and comes with a long warranty, making it a popular choice for kitchens, hallways, and living areas.

Care depends on the flooring type. For Amtico and vinyl, regular sweeping and occasional mopping with a pH-neutral cleaner is sufficient. Hardwood floors benefit from specialist wood cleaners and felt pads on furniture. We provide detailed aftercare advice with every installation.
