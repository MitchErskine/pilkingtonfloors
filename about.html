About Pilkington Floors

Over 15 years of passion, precision, and premium flooring — serving homes and businesses across Greater Manchester and beyond.

Our Story

From humble beginnings to one of the region's most trusted flooring specialists.

Pilkington Floors was founded with a simple belief: that every home and business deserves flooring that is both beautiful and built to last. What started as a small, local operation driven by a genuine passion for craftsmanship has grown into a trusted name across Greater Manchester and the surrounding areas.

With over 15 years of hands-on experience, we have built our reputation one project at a time — from cosy living rooms and busy family kitchens to large-scale commercial fit-outs. Our roots are firmly local, and we take pride in the relationships we have built with homeowners, landlords, and business owners throughout the community.

Every member of our team shares the same commitment to quality and attention to detail that defined our very first installation. We do not cut corners, and we treat every property as if it were our own. That is the Pilkington Floors difference.

Amtico Approved Installers

Specialist training, premium products, and a partnership built on excellence.

Pilkington Floors is proud to be an approved Amtico installer — a distinction that reflects our specialist training, proven skill, and commitment to the highest installation standards. Amtico is one of the world's leading luxury vinyl tile brands, renowned for its stunning designs, exceptional durability, and long-lasting performance.

As approved installers, we have direct access to the full Amtico range, including the Signature, Form, and Spacia collections. We can help you explore hundreds of designs — from realistic wood and stone effects to bold geometric patterns and custom borders — and bring your vision to life with precision fitting.

Choosing an approved installer means your flooring is fitted to Amtico's exacting standards, protecting your warranty and ensuring a flawless finish. Whether it is a classic herringbone hallway or a contemporary open-plan living space, our Amtico expertise guarantees a result you will love for years to come.
