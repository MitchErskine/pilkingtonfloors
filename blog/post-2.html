How to Choose the Right Flooring for Your Home

Choosing new flooring is one of the biggest decisions you will make when renovating your home. The right floor can transform a room, add value to your property, and last for decades. But with so many materials, styles, and price points available, it can be difficult to know where to start.

In this guide, we break down the key factors to consider so you can make a confident, informed choice.

Consider the Room

Different rooms have different demands. The flooring that works perfectly in a bedroom may not be suitable for a kitchen or bathroom. Here is a quick overview:

  • Kitchens and bathrooms: These rooms need waterproof or water-resistant flooring. Vinyl, Amtico, and ceramic tiles are all strong choices. Avoid untreated hardwood, which can warp with moisture exposure.
  • Living rooms and dining rooms: Hardwood, engineered wood, and Amtico all work well here. These rooms benefit from flooring that is both attractive and durable enough for daily use.
  • Bedrooms: Comfort is the priority. Carpet is a popular choice for its warmth and softness, though many homeowners now opt for hardwood or laminate with a rug for a cleaner look.
  • Hallways and stairs: High-traffic areas need hard-wearing flooring. Amtico, laminate, and engineered hardwood are all excellent options that can handle constant footfall.

Think About Your Lifestyle

Your household makeup plays a big role in which flooring will work best:

  • Families with young children: Look for flooring that is easy to clean, scratch-resistant, and comfortable. Amtico and vinyl are practical choices that still look great.
  • Pet owners: Pets can be tough on floors. Avoid soft woods that scratch easily. Amtico, laminate, and luxury vinyl are all pet-friendly options.
  • Allergy sufferers: Hard flooring is easier to keep free of dust and allergens than carpet. Hardwood, vinyl, and Amtico are all good choices for allergy-prone households.

Set a Realistic Budget

Flooring costs vary significantly depending on the material, quality, and installation complexity. Here is a rough guide to help you plan:

  • Budget-friendly: Laminate and standard vinyl offer great value and come in a wide range of styles. They are quick to install and easy to maintain.
  • Mid-range: Engineered hardwood and Amtico Spacia sit in the mid-range. They offer a premium look and feel without the price tag of solid wood or Amtico Signature.
  • Premium: Solid hardwood and Amtico Signature are at the higher end. They offer unmatched quality, design flexibility, and longevity.

Remember to factor in the cost of installation, underlay (where needed), and any subfloor preparation. A professional installation ensures your flooring performs as intended and lasts as long as possible.

Comparing Flooring Materials

Here is a quick comparison of the most popular flooring types to help you weigh up the pros and cons:

  • Hardwood: Beautiful and long-lasting, but requires more maintenance and is sensitive to moisture. Best for living areas and bedrooms.
  • Laminate: Affordable and durable with a wide range of styles. Not waterproof, so avoid in wet rooms. Good for bedrooms, living rooms, and hallways.
  • Vinyl / LVT: Waterproof, comfortable underfoot, and available in realistic wood and stone effects. Suitable for every room in the house.
  • Amtico: A premium LVT with superior design options and durability. Ideal for homeowners who want the look of natural materials with minimal upkeep.
  • Carpet: Warm, soft, and available in countless colours and textures. Best for bedrooms and living rooms. Requires regular vacuuming and occasional deep cleaning.
