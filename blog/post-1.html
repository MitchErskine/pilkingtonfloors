The Complete Guide to Amtico Flooring

What Is Amtico Flooring?

Amtico is a premium luxury vinyl tile (LVT) brand manufactured in the UK. It replicates the look and feel of natural materials — including wood, stone, and marble — using advanced photographic and embossing technology. Each tile is made from multiple layers that provide durability, water resistance, and a realistic surface texture.

Unlike standard vinyl, Amtico tiles are individually cut and laid in patterns such as herringbone, basketweave, and running bond. This gives homeowners the aesthetic of real wood or stone flooring without the associated maintenance challenges.

Benefits of Amtico

There are several reasons Amtico has become one of the most popular flooring choices for UK homes and businesses:

  • Durability: Amtico floors are built to withstand heavy foot traffic, making them suitable for hallways, kitchens, and commercial spaces. Most products come with a 20-year or lifetime residential warranty.
  • Water resistance: Unlike hardwood, Amtico is fully waterproof. This makes it an excellent choice for bathrooms, kitchens, and utility rooms.
  • Low maintenance: Regular sweeping and occasional mopping with a pH-neutral cleaner is all that is needed to keep Amtico looking its best.
  • Design versatility: With hundreds of colours, textures, and laying patterns available, Amtico can be tailored to suit any interior style — from rustic farmhouse to sleek contemporary.
  • Comfort underfoot: Amtico is warmer and softer underfoot than ceramic tile or natural stone, and it is compatible with underfloor heating systems.

Popular Amtico Designs

Amtico offers several product ranges to suit different budgets and tastes:

  • Amtico Signature: The flagship collection with the widest range of designs and laying patterns. Ideal for creating bespoke floors with borders and feature strips.
  • Amtico Spacia: A versatile mid-range option offering realistic wood and stone effects at a more accessible price point.
  • Amtico Form: A contemporary collection with clean lines and modern colourways, designed for quick and easy installation.
  • Amtico Click Smart: A click-fit system that can be installed without adhesive, making it suitable for DIY projects or rental properties.

Caring for Your Amtico Floor

Proper care will keep your Amtico floor looking new for years. Here are our top maintenance tips:

  • Sweep or vacuum regularly to remove dust and grit that can cause surface scratches.
  • Mop with a damp cloth or microfibre mop using a pH-neutral floor cleaner. Avoid harsh chemicals, bleach, or abrasive cleaners.
  • Place felt pads under furniture legs to prevent indentation marks.
  • Use doormats at entrances to reduce the amount of dirt tracked onto the floor.
  • Wipe up spills promptly — while Amtico is waterproof, standing water can seep into seams over time.

Amtico also offers its own range of cleaning products specifically formulated for their floors, which we recommend for the best results.

Is Amtico Right for You?

If you are looking for a flooring solution that combines the beauty of natural materials with practical durability, Amtico is an excellent choice. It works well in virtually every room of the house and is equally suited to commercial environments such as offices, shops, and restaurants.
